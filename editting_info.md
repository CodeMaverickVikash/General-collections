# Media Editing Software & Tools

Recommended free and open-source applications for audio, video, and photo production:

| Tool | Category | Primary Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GIMP** | Photo Editing | Full-featured image editing and manipulation (Photoshop alternative) |
| **Kdenlive** / **DaVinci Resolve** | Video Editing | Timeline video editing, transitions, and color grading |
| **OBS Studio** | Recording & Streaming | High-performance screen capture and live broadcasting |
| **Audacity** | Audio Editing | Audio cleanup, voiceover enhancement, and noise reduction |
| **HandBrake** | Video Compression | Fast video transcoding and final output size reduction |
