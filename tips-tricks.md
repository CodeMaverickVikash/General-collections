# Developer Tips & Tricks

## Standup & Work Log Keywords
Useful professional phrases for daily standups, Jira work logs, and timesheets:
- *Troubleshoot issue.*
- *Debug the issues.*
- *Gathered and understood the requirements.*
- *Analysis and technical discussion.*

---

## Chrome DevTools Tip
- **Sources Panel Auto-focus:**  
  Toggle `"focus sources panel when triggering a breakpoint"` in DevTools Settings depending on whether you want breakpoint hits to automatically switch tabs.

---

## Workspace & Ergonomics
- **Display Brightness:** Keep laptop night brightness around **30%–40%** to minimize eye strain during extended work hours.
